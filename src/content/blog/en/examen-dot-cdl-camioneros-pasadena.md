If you drive commercial, the DOT physical is the only thing standing between your license and a week off the road. Most drivers who come to us in Pasadena walk in with the same question: "am I going to pass?". This guide explains exactly what gets measured, with what numbers, and what you can do before the visit so nothing catches you by surprise.

## What the exam checks

The exam follows [FMCSA](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/medical) rules and covers:

- **Vision:** at least 20/40 in each eye and both together, with glasses if you wear them, plus a 70-degree field of vision in each eye. You also need to distinguish traffic signal colors.
- **Hearing:** you must hear a forced whisper at five feet, or pass an audiometer test.
- **Blood pressure.** The number one reason a certificate comes back short. Under 140/90 the certificate can run 24 months; between 140/90 and 159/99 it is usually one year; higher than that, the term gets shorter and medical follow-up is required.
- **Urinalysis**, which looks for signs of kidney or sugar problems. It is not a drug test: that is separate.
- **Medical history and medications**, including sleep apnea, diabetes and any recent surgery.

## What to bring

1. **Photo ID** and your CDL, or your permit if you are getting one.
2. **Glasses or hearing aids**, if you use them. Without them, corrected vision cannot be certified.
3. **A list of your medications**, with doses.
4. **If you take blood pressure medication**, recent readings help.
5. **If you use insulin**, the [MCSA-5870](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/medical/driver-medical-requirements/insulin-treated-diabetes-mellitus-assessment-form-mcsa-5870) form filled out by the provider who manages your diabetes.
6. **If you use CPAP**, your compliance report for the last 90 days.

## How to show up in better shape

Three things that are in your hands and change the outcome of the day:

- **No coffee or energy drinks.** They raise your pressure right before it gets measured. Same with smoking in the hour before.
- **Sleep and eat normally.** Arriving fasted after many hours behind the wheel usually gives worse readings.
- **If you know your pressure runs high**, take your medication as usual and come early, not after a twelve-hour shift.

## How long the certificate lasts

The maximum is **24 months**. With controlled high blood pressure, diabetes or another condition under follow-up, the examiner may issue it for 12 months or less. That is not a punishment: it is the window to check you again.

After the exam, remember that in Texas the certificate is reported to the state DPS so your license stays active. Always keep your copy.
